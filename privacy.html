Privacy Policy

Downforce Lab · Last updated: 21 October 2026

Downforce Lab is a private automation tool used only by the owner of the Downforce Lab YouTube channel (@downforcelabyt) to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to other users.

What Google data the app accesses

  • YouTube: uploading the channel's own videos and setting their title, description, tags, privacy status and publish time.
  • Google Sheets / Drive: reading and updating one spreadsheet that the owner created to plan video topics.

The app does not access any other user's data, and it does not read the owner's other files, emails or account details.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish videos to the owner's channel and keep the topic spreadsheet up to date. Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own server and are never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Downforce Lab's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
